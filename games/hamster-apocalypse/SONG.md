# 🎸 WHEEK AND ROLL (feat. the Zombie Apocalypse)

*The official rock anthem of Central Park Last Stand.*

140 BPM · Key of E · **Instrumental** — procedurally played live in `src/music.js`
(drum kit + driving square-wave bass + detuned power chords, compressed as
one band). No vocals, no squeaks — just the band.

**Controls:** the soundtrack starts with the game, **[M]** stops / resumes it,
and pausing the game stops it too.

## The loop (32 bars, ~55 s)

| Bars | Riff | Feel |
|------|------|------|
| 1–8  | E · E · C · G | full 8th-note chug, open hats on the beats |
| 9–16 | E · G · C · B | the hook — tight closed 8th hats |
| 17–24| G · G · C · B | higher-root chug, open hats again |
| 25–32| E · G · C · B | the hook once more, 16th-note snare fill to close |

*(The original vocal — “WHEEK AND ROLL! UP THE WALL!” — was cut at the
band's request. The hamster prefers to scream quietly on the couch.)*
